How to Generate an App Password

Generate a secure 16-character password to connect your mailbox

  1. Log in to your Zoho Account at accounts.zoho.in.
  2. Navigate to the Security section from the left navigation menu.
  3. Scroll down to App Passwords and click Generate New Password.
  4. Enter HR Flow as the App Name, then click Generate.
  5. Copy the generated 16-digit password (spaces optional) and paste it into the App Password field.
